# CP29 — 4.1 System of controls – product traceability and integrity

**Official checking-point text:**
from the property (if any) from which they were transferred to receival into the establishment

**Why these sections govern this checking point:**
Section 4-7B(a) expressly requires that prescribed plants and plant products prepared at a registered establishment must be traced 'from the property (if any) from which they were transferred to the registered establishment', which is exactly the receival-into-the-establishment leg of traceability that CP29 checks.

# Section 4-7B — Plants and plant products must be traced

4-7B  Plants and plant products must be traced 
 
 Prescribed plants and plant products prepared at a registered establishment must 
be traced: 
 
(a) from the property (if any) from which they were transferred to the 
registered establishment; and 
 
(b) while they are at the registered establishment; and 
 
(c) to the premises to which they are transferred from the registered 
establishment.
