# CP41 — 4.4 Importing country requirements

**Official checking-point text:**
Where applicable, all importing country requirements relating to the export operations carried out at a registered establishment in relation to prescribed plants or plant products and the plants or plant products must be met.

**Why these sections govern this checking point:**
Section 4-7 states that 'all importing country requirements relating to the export operations carried out at a registered establishment in relation to prescribed plants or plant products and the plants or plant products must be met', which is a verbatim match to CP41's requirement.
