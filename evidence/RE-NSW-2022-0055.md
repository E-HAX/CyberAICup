Rules s4-2(5), s4-2(6) — Element 2.2 checking points C4, C5, C6

The receival and processing building is an original structure built circa 2000. Internal walls are rough-finished brick with lime render — surface is porous, absorbing product oils and moisture. Design features minimising pest entry: smooth wall surfaces; all roof-wall junctions factory-sealed; cable/pipe penetrations sealed with food-grade silicone; 6 mm mesh on all fixed ventilation louvres; self-closing personnel doors with magnetic seals; roller doors serviced annually to maintain threshold seal integrity; no open drainage channels.
5.3  Waste Disposal Design (C6)
Contained waste management infrastructure: liquid drains to bunded sump (2000 L) outside the processing building; solid waste in sealed wheeled bins with lids; external waste bay roofed and bunded on three sides with 300 mm concrete kerb and sealed slab. Organic waste composted in covered bay 30 m from processing. No open waste storage areas.

5.  AREA-SPECIFIC CLEANING PROCEDURES (SOPs)
Detailed step-by-step procedures for each area of the establishment
Receival Pit / Weighbridge
Pre-receival: Inspect pit and scale platform for residual grain from previous lot. Activate compressed air lance to dislodge grain from crevices, joints and conveyor housings. Sweep accumulated grain into collection bin. Wipe scale display and control panel with damp cloth. Supervisor to sign pre-loading checklist before grain intake commences.
Elevator Boot & Leg Casings
At end of each operating day: Lock out elevator drive before opening access hatch. Using industrial vacuum, extract all dead-stock grain from boot throat, casing joints, and transition points. Inspect internal surfaces for grain bridges or insect webbing. Record any pest signs in the Pest Monitoring Log. Replace and secure access hatch. Enter completed task in Equipment Cleaning Log.
Belt Conveyors & Augers
Weekly maintenance cleaning: Isolate drive motor and lock out before commencing. Run conveyor empty for five minutes to clear carrying strand. Remove belt guards and clean all carry rollers and return rollers with a stiff brush. Vacuum accumulated grain and dust from trough pan. Inspect belt splice and edges for wear. Reassemble all guards before returning to service. Record in Cleaning Log.
Grain Storage Silos / Bins
Between commodity lots: Full physical inspection and clean-out required before loading a new grain lot. Access silo through roof hatch or side door (confined space procedure applies). Sweep-out all surfaces using long-handled broom. Vacuum residue from cone and floor. Inspect walls and roof for webbing, insect activity, or mould. Record findings on Pre-Loading Checklist. Silo must not be loaded until the checklist is signed by the Storage Manager.
Grain Screening / Cleaning Plant
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Grain Screening / Cleaning Plant in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
6.  MANAGEMENT OF RESIDUES AND DEAD-STOCK ZONES
Particular attention is given to dead-stock zones in elevator boots, conveyor transition points, and auger troughs. These areas accumulate grain that cannot self-clear during normal operation and provide harbouring conditions for stored product insects. Full physical extraction is required at each lot change.
