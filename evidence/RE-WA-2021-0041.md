6. Hygiene and Waste Controls (Checking Point 3.1)
Hygiene and Sanitation Program (EF-HSP-002):
• Pre-harvest: all silo bins cleaned, swept and bait-bag tested before intake.
• Equipment: grain elevator, conveyors and scalper cleaned between commodity changes (compressed air + brush).
• Cleaning schedule: weekly sweep of receival pit floor; monthly wash of elevator boot; bi-annual pressure wash of silo interiors between crops.
• Personnel hygiene: no-food-in-grain-area policy; handwashing facilities at site entry; PPE (dust masks, gloves) provided.
• Records: Cleaning register (Form GCE-CLN-01) signed after each event.
Waste Management Procedure (EF-WMP-001):
• Screenings: collected in sealed bins; removed by licensed carrier to approved composting facility weekly.
• Grain dust: dust extraction unit emptied into sealed skip; disposed with screenings.
• Chemical waste (empty fumigant sachets, bait material): disposed per APVMA label instructions; manifest retained.
• Liquid waste (lubricants, hydraulic oil): collected in drip trays; removed by licensed liquid waste contractor.
• Waste storage area: segregated from grain storage; labelled; covered to prevent vermin access.
7. Pest Control (Checking Point 3.2)
Pest Control Program (EF-PCP-001) — Integrated Pest Management:
• Rodent control: 18 perimeter bait stations (Bromadiolone, tamper-resistant boxes), mapped in Bait Station Plan.
• Insect control: phosphine fumigation of stored grain when insect activity detected by pitfall traps.
• Bird exclusion: all silo vents and openings bird-proofed; netting installed at conveyor gallery ends.
• Monitoring: monthly visual inspection of all bait stations (Form GCE-PC-01); bi-monthly pitfall trap check; grain temperature probe weekly.
• Licensed pest contractor: Annual review by APC-licensed contractor; report filed.
8. Traceability and Integrity (Checking Point 4.1)
Traceability System (GCE-TRACE-001):
• One step back: each silo assigned a unique ID containing grower name, paddock ID, variety, harvest year (e.g., GCE-SILO3-SMITH-PADD2-APW1-2025).
• One step forward: each dispatch docket links silo ID → transport unit rego → receiving establishment.
• Batch records: receival docket → lab test result → silo allocation → fumigation certificate → dispatch docket, all linked by lot reference.
• Records retained 2 years minimum. Digital copies in FarmWizard grain management system.
9. Product Flow Chart and Failed Goods (Checking Point 4.1)
Product flow chart: see Section 3 Process Flow Diagram.
Failed Goods Management Procedure (GCE-FGP-001):
• If a lot fails mycotoxin, pesticide or ergot tests: segregate immediately; label silo as 'HOLD'; notify Quality Manager.
• Blending of failed lots with passing lots to achieve compliance is NOT permitted for mycotoxin or ergot failures.
• Re-testing: one re-test from additional sample permitted if initial result near threshold; documented.
• Downgrade: failed export lots may be sold as domestic feed grain after grower consultation.
• Rejected at port: consignment returned to registered establishment; re-inspect; document non-conformance; notify DAFF.
10. Phytosanitary Security / Contamination Risk (Checking Point 4.2)
Phytosanitary Security (GCE-PSP-001):
• After fumigation, grain remains in sealed silo until DAFF sampling. No grain movement between sampling and loading.
• Transport unit inspected for cleanliness and freedom from infestation before loading (Form GCE-TU-01).
• Sealed containers: seal number recorded on dispatch docket; communicated to receiving port.
• Co-mingling of export-cleared grain with non-cleared grain is prohibited at all times.

5.  AREA-SPECIFIC CLEANING PROCEDURES (SOPs)
Detailed step-by-step procedures for each area of the establishment
Receival Pit / Weighbridge
Pre-receival: Inspect pit and scale platform for residual grain from previous lot. Activate compressed air lance to dislodge grain from crevices, joints and conveyor housings. Sweep accumulated grain into collection bin. Wipe scale display and control panel with damp cloth. Supervisor to sign pre-loading checklist before grain intake commences.
Elevator Boot & Leg Casings
At end of each operating day: Lock out elevator drive before opening access hatch. Using industrial vacuum, extract all dead-stock grain from boot throat, casing joints, and transition points. Inspect internal surfaces for grain bridges or insect webbing. Record any pest signs in the Pest Monitoring Log. Replace and secure access hatch. Enter completed task in Equipment Cleaning Log.
Belt Conveyors & Augers
Weekly maintenance cleaning: Isolate drive motor and lock out before commencing. Run conveyor empty for five minutes to clear carrying strand. Remove belt guards and clean all carry rollers and return rollers with a stiff brush. Vacuum accumulated grain and dust from trough pan. Inspect belt splice and edges for wear. Reassemble all guards before returning to service. Record in Cleaning Log.
Grain Storage Silos / Bins
Between commodity lots: Full physical inspection and clean-out required before loading a new grain lot. Access silo through roof hatch or side door (confined space procedure applies). Sweep-out all surfaces using long-handled broom. Vacuum residue from cone and floor. Inspect walls and roof for webbing, insect activity, or mould. Record findings on Pre-Loading Checklist. Silo must not be loaded until the checklist is signed by the Storage Manager.
Grain Screening / Cleaning Plant
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Grain Screening / Cleaning Plant in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
6.  MANAGEMENT OF RESIDUES AND DEAD-STOCK ZONES
Particular attention is given to dead-stock zones in elevator boots, conveyor transition points, and auger troughs. These areas accumulate grain that cannot self-clear during normal operation and provide harbouring conditions for stored product insects. Full physical extraction is required at each lot change.
