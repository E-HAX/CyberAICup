# CP36 — 4.2 System of controls – phytosanitary security

**Official checking-point text:**
minimising the risk of substitution (switching of goods)

**Why these sections govern this checking point:**
Section 4-8 requires that 'the integrity of prescribed plants or plant products prepared at a registered establishment must be ensured while the plants or plant products are at the establishment', and product integrity is the concept that covers preventing unauthorised switching/substitution of goods described in CP36.

**Governing policy text** (concatenated from the cited sections above):

---

# Section 4-8 — Integrity

4-8  Integrity 
 
 The integrity of prescribed plants or plant products prepared at a registered 
establishment must be ensured while the plants or plant products are at the 
establishment.
