**Why these sections govern this checking point:**
Section 4-7B(b) requires that the plants or plant products must be traced 'while they are at the registered establishment', directly imposing the on-site traceability obligation described by CP30.

# Section 4-7B — Plants and plant products must be traced

4-7B  Plants and plant products must be traced 
 
 Prescribed plants and plant products prepared at a registered establishment must 
be traced: 
 
(a) from the property (if any) from which they were transferred to the 
registered establishment; and 
 
(b) while they are at the registered establishment; and 
 
(c) to the premises to which they are transferred from the registered 
establishment.
