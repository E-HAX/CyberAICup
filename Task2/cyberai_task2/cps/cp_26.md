# CP26 — 3.5 Establishment condition – pest control

**Official checking-point text:**
Where applicable, pest control stations and traps (for example, rodent bait stations) are fit for purpose and in good working order.

**Why these sections govern this checking point:**
Section 4-7A(2)(b) requires pests and contaminants at the establishment to be controlled in a manner appropriate for the export operations (which includes devices such as bait stations and traps being fit for purpose), and section 4-6 makes the equipment and facilities requirements of section 4-2 (including that equipment be adequate to ensure the requirements of the Act are met) continue to apply to a registered establishment, together supporting the obligation that pest-control equipment be fit for purpose and in good working order.

# Section 4-7A — Establishment must be kept in appropriate condition

4-7A  Establishment must be kept in appropriate condition 
 
(1) A registered establishment must be kept in a condition: 
 
(a) appropriate for the export operations carried out at the establishment and 
the plants or plant products at the property; and 
 
(b) that ensures that prescribed plants and plant products at the establishment 
are not, or not likely to be, infested by pests or contaminated. 
Example: Materials at the establishment must be monitored, controlled and maintained so that 
they do not contaminate plants or plant products, and are not likely to harbour pests 
that could infest plants or plant products, at the establishment.

(a) the establishment must be kept in a clean and hygienic condition 
appropriate for the export operations carried out at the establishment and

# Section 4-6 — Construction, equipment and facilities

4-6  Construction, equipment and facilities 
 
 The requirements for registration of an establishment prescribed by section 4-2 
must continue to be met in relation to a registered establishment after it is 
registered. 
Note: 
Alterations of a registered establishment (including additions to the establishment, but 
not including alterations prescribed by section 4-16) must not be made unless the 
alterations have been approved under subsection 120(2) of the Act and the occupier of 
the establishment has been given notice of the approval under section 121 of the Act.
