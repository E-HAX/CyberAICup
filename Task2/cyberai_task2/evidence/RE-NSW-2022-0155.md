5.  AREA-SPECIFIC CLEANING PROCEDURES (SOPs)
Detailed step-by-step procedures for each area of the establishment
Receival / Intake Hopper
After each intake run: Lock out receiving conveyor. Use compressed air lance to dislodge pulse residue from hopper walls, conveyor trough, and all transition points. Vacuum accumulated screenings and fine material. Inspect underside of conveyor belt for product trapping. Record in Intake Checklist.
Cleaner / Aspirator / Colour Sorter
After each cleaning run: Remove accumulated screenings from collection trays and hoppers. Brush aspirator screens with dedicated brush to remove lodged seeds. For colour sorter: follow manufacturer's lens cleaning protocol. Vacuum sensor housings. Inspect all filters and replace if blocked. Record in Plant Operator Run Log.
Storage Silos / Bins
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Storage Silos / Bins in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
Packing Line / Bagging Machine
End of each shift: Clear all pulse product from the line. Vacuum or brush conveyor belts, weigh hoppers, and bag former guides to remove pulse fines. Prepare ProClean 200 at 1:50. Wipe external surfaces of bagging machine and packing bench. Check sealing jaws and heat elements for residue. Record in Shift Log.
Palletising Area / Loading Dock
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Palletising Area / Loading Dock in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
6.  MANAGEMENT OF RESIDUES AND DEAD-STOCK ZONES
Dead-stock accumulation occurs in aspirator collection trays, conveyor trough joints, and under the bagging machine floor plate. These areas are vacuumed or blown out at each run change to prevent insect harbourage.
