6. Hygiene and Waste Controls (Checking Point 3.1)
Hygiene and Sanitation Program (DDPE-HSP-001):
• Pre-season: full clean-down of receival pit, conveyors, pre-cleaner, gravity table, colour sorter and packing lines.
• During season: colour sorter air nozzles cleaned daily; packing tables sanitised between shifts.
• Personnel: handwash on entry; no food in processing area; dust masks and gloves provided.
• Records: Cleaning Register (DDPE-CLN-01) signed after each event.
Waste Management Procedure (DDPE-WMP-001):
• Pre-cleaner trash / weed seeds: collected in sealed bins; removed to composting weekly.
• Dust: cyclone collector emptied weekly; disposed to composting.
• Reject bags: damaged or misprinted bags recycled through approved contractor.
• Chemical waste: fumigant containers disposed per APVMA label; manifest maintained.
7. Pest Control (Checking Point 3.2)
Pest Control Program (DDPE-PCP-001):
• Rodents: 14 perimeter bait stations; monthly inspection; licensed contractor quarterly.
• Insects: pitfall traps in sealed shed; phosphine fumigation when insects detected.
• Bird exclusion: netting at loading bay; doors kept closed.
• Records: Pest Control Log (DDPE-PC-01) monthly.

5.  AREA-SPECIFIC CLEANING PROCEDURES (SOPs)
Detailed step-by-step procedures for each area of the establishment
Receival / Intake Area
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Receival / Intake Area in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
Huller / Cracker / Sheller
After each production run: Power off and lock out drive before opening. Dismantle outer access panels. Remove nut residue, shell fragments, and skin from rollers, screens, and internal surfaces using a stiff brush. Prepare BioDegreaser FD at 1:25. Apply to metal surfaces where oil residue is present. Contact time 5 minutes. Rinse with water. Allow to dry before reassembly. Record in Equipment Cleaning Log.
Grading & Sizing Equipment
After each run: Clear all nut product from grading screens and conveyor belts. Brush screens using designated nut-cleaning brush to remove embedded skins and fragments. Prepare EnzymeClear at 1:20 in a spray bottle. Spray conveyor belt and rollers. Wipe with clean cloth. Inspect for any remaining organic residue. Record in Run Record.
Drying Area / Pads / Dryer
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Drying Area / Pads / Dryer in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
Storage Bins / Holding Area
Between commodity lots: Inspect and clean storage bins before loading a new nut commodity. Using industrial vacuum, remove all residual nut material from bin walls, seams, and floor. Inspect for pest activity (insect frass, rodent droppings). Storage Manager signs pre-loading checklist confirming bin is clean and free of pests.
6.  MANAGEMENT OF RESIDUES AND DEAD-STOCK ZONES
Dead-stock zones include the inner drum of hulling and cracking equipment, under-belt spaces on grading conveyors, and the floor beneath dryer trays. Nut oil residue and fine skin particles accumulate in these areas and require periodic disassembly cleaning.
