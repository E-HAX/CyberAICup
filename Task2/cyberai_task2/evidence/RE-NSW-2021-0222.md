5.  AREA-SPECIFIC CLEANING PROCEDURES (SOPs)
Detailed step-by-step procedures for each area of the establishment
Receival / Intake Area
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Receival / Intake Area in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
Processing Equipment / Line
After each production run: Isolate equipment before cleaning. Remove product residue from all contact surfaces: rollers, belts, screens. Prepare ProClean 200 at 1:50. Apply to surfaces, contact time 3 minutes, rinse with clean water. For heavily soiled components, use BioDegreaser FD at 1:25 prior to main clean. Inspect all surfaces for residue before returning to production. Record in Equipment Log.
Drying / Conditioning Area
After each drying cycle: Allow drying surfaces to cool before cleaning. Use a stiff brush and compressed air to remove product residue and fines from drying mesh, trays, or belts. Vacuum accumulated material from under dryer frame. Inspect moisture sensing equipment surfaces. Record in Drying Record.
Storage / Holding Area
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Storage / Holding Area in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
Packing & Export Preparation Area
End of each shift: Clear all product from packing tables and export staging area. Remove used packaging materials to waste bay. Prepare ProClean 200 at 1:50. Wipe packing tables, scales, and conveyor surfaces. Sweep and mop floor. Confirm area is clear and clean before locking up. Record in Shift Cleaning Log.

Product fines and residues accumulate in transition points, screen joins, and under drying surfaces. These dead zones are addressed during each production run clean-down and inspected during the weekly walkthrough.
