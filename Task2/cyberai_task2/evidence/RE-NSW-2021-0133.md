5.  AREA-SPECIFIC CLEANING PROCEDURES (SOPs)
Detailed step-by-step procedures for each area of the establishment
Receival Dock / Unloading Bay
After each produce intake: Remove all packing material (bins, cartons, netting) from dock. Sweep floor of organic debris (leaves, soil, rejected produce). Prepare ProClean 200 at 1:50. Apply to floor with scrubbing brush, allow 3-minute contact time, then rinse with pressure hose ensuring all residue is cleared to drain. Check dock door seals for damage. Sign intake log on completion.
Grading & Sorting Line
After each production run: Power down and isolate grading line before cleaning. Remove produce residue from conveyor belts and rollers manually. Prepare EnzymeClear at 1:20. Apply to belt surfaces, brushes, and rollers. Contact time 5 minutes. Wipe down all surfaces and rinse with clean water. Inspect brushes for wear or product trapping. Restart test run to confirm mechanical function. Record in Grading Log.
Packing Area / Line
End of each shift: Clear all produce from packing tables and conveyor line. Remove used packaging film, cartons, and waste to the external waste bay. Prepare ProClean 200 at 1:50. Wipe packing tables, scales, and conveyor surfaces. Sweep and mop floor. Check under conveyor for accumulation. Packing Supervisor signs shift cleaning record before end of shift.
Cold Storage / Coolrooms
Monthly and between commodity loads: Defrost evaporator coils per manufacturer instructions. Prepare AcidRinse 500 at 1:50. Apply to evaporator surfaces and drain tray to remove mineral scale. Rinse. Prepare QuatSan 100 at 1:100. Wipe all internal wall panels, shelving, and floor. Clear floor drains of accumulated organic material. Record temperature before re-loading. Cold Store Manager signs maintenance log.
Pre-Cool Chamber
Designated staff are responsible for maintaining the Pre-Cool Chamber in a clean and hygienic condition following each production run or daily as scheduled. Appropriate cleaning agents at specified dilutions shall be used. Completed cleaning activities are to be recorded in the site cleaning log.
6.  MANAGEMENT OF RESIDUES AND DEAD-STOCK ZONES
Areas prone to organic residue accumulation include floor drain channels, conveyor return runs, and spaces under grading machinery. These areas are cleaned daily or as required to prevent fermentation, mould growth, and pest attraction.
