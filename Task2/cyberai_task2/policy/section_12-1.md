# Section 12-1 — Definitions

12-1  Definitions 
 
 In this Chapter: 
commencement time means the time when section 3 of the Export Control Act 
2020 commences. 
old Export Control (General) Order means the Export Control (Prescribed 
Goods—General) Order 2005, as in force immediately before the 
commencement time. 
old Export Control (Plants) Order means the Export Control (Plants and Plant 
Products) Order 2011, as in force immediately before the commencement time.
