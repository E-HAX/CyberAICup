# Section 9-7 — Purpose of this Division

9-7  Purpose of this Division 
 
 For the purposes of section 279 of the Act, this Division makes provision for and 
in relation to the following matters: 
 
(a) carrying out assessments of plants or plant products; 
 
(b) the process to be followed after an assessment of plants or plant products 
has been completed; 
 
(c) records of assessments of plants or plant products; 
 
(d) other matters relating to assessments of plants or plant products.
