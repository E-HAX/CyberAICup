# Section 2-5 — Application of this Part

2-5  Application of this Part 
 
 This Part applies in relation to prescribed plants or plant products (in this 
Part called relevant goods). 
Note 1: 
See Division 1 of Part 1 of this Chapter in relation to goods that are prescribed plants or 
plant products. 
Note 2: 
Plants and plant products are taken not to be prescribed goods in the circumstances 
prescribed by section 2-2 (see the definition of prescribed goods in section 12 of the 
Act).
