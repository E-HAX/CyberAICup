# Section 9-3 — Purpose of this Division

9-3  Purpose of this Division 
 
 For the purposes of subsections 270(4) and (5) of the Act, this Division makes 
provision for and in relation to the following matters: 
 
(a) the conduct of an audit; 
 
(b) processes for dealing with any non-compliance with a requirement to 
which an audit relates; 
 
(c) audit reports.
