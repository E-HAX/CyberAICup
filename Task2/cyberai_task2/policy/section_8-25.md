# Section 8-25 — Alteration of and interference with official marks

8-25  Alteration of and interference with official marks 
 
 A person may alter, or interfere with, an official mark (whether or not it has been 
applied to any plants or plant products) only if the alteration or interference is 
required or permitted by this Part or the Secretary. 
Note: 
A person may commit an offence or be liable to a civil penalty if the person engages in 
conduct and the conduct has the result that an official mark applied to certain plants or 
plant products or documents is altered so as to be false, misleading or deceptive (see 
sections 261 and 262 of the Act).
