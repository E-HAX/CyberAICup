# Section 11-12 — Storage of samples

11-12  Storage of samples 
 
(1) For the purposes of section 411 of the Act, a sample that may be tested or 
analysed under the Act must be held under conditions that are unlikely to affect 
the result of any testing or analysis of the sample. 
 
(2) Subsection (1) does not apply in relation to a sample that may be tested or 
analysed in the performance of functions or duties or the exercise of powers 
under Chapter 10 of the Act (compliance and enforcement) or the Regulatory 
Powers Act.
