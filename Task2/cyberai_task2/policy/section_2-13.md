# Section 2-13 — When phytosanitary certificate, or phytosanitary certificate for re-export,

2-13  When phytosanitary certificate, or phytosanitary certificate for re-export, 
in relation to non-prescribed plants or plant products ceases to be in 
force 
 
 For the purposes of paragraph 72(4)(c) of the Act, a phytosanitary certificate, or 
a phytosanitary certificate for re-export, in relation to non-prescribed plants or 
plant products that have not been exported ceases to be in force if the validity 
period for the plants or plant products has ended.
