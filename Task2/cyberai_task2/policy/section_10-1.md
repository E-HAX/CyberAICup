# Section 10-1 — Samples taken in exercising monitoring or investigation powers

10-1  Samples taken in exercising monitoring or investigation powers 
 
(1) This section applies if a sample is taken as permitted by paragraph 327(2)(a) of 
the Act (additional monitoring power) or subsection 330(2) of the Act (additional 
investigation power). 
 
(2) The sample must be: 
 
(a) identified with a mark or a tag; and 
 
(b) kept under conditions that are unlikely to affect the result of any testing or 
analysis of the sample; and 
 
(c) kept in the custody or control of an authorised officer until whichever of 
the following first occurs: 
 
(i) the sample is given to an analyst appointed under section 413 of the 
Act; 
 
(ii) the sample is destroyed during testing or analysis in accordance with 
section 412 of the Act; 
 
(iii) the sample is otherwise disposed of.
