# Section 9-19A — Condition of authorisation—bulk vessel inspections

9-19A  Condition of authorisation—bulk vessel inspections 
 
 For the purposes of paragraph 292(1)(a) of the Act, it is a condition of the 
authorisation of a third party authorised officer who may carry out inspections of 
bulk vessels that the third party authorised officer must make a video recording 
of each inspection carried out by the third party authorised officer.
