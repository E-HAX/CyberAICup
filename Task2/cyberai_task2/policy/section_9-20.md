9-20  Functions and powers of authorised officers—bulk vessel approvals and 
container approvals

provisions that confer functions and powers on authorised officers in relation to 
bulk vessel approvals.

Note: 
An authorised officer may only perform functions or exercise powers conferred on an 
authorised officer by the Act that are specified in the authorised officer’s instrument of 
authorisation (see subsection 301(1) of the Act).
