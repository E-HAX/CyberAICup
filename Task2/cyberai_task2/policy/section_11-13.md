# Section 11-13 — Division of compensation between owners

11-13  Division of compensation between owners 
 
 For the purposes of paragraph 420(2)(b) of the Act, compensation in respect of 
plants or plant products that are owned by 2 or more owners must be divided 
among those owners so that each owner is paid an amount of compensation that 
is equal to the proportion that the Secretary is satisfied represents the owner’s 
interest in the plants or plant products at the time the plants or plant products 
were damaged or destroyed.
