# Section 12-8 — Person approved before commencement time to possess an official marking

12-8  Person approved before commencement time to possess an official marking 
device 
 
(1) This section applies in relation to a person who, immediately before the 
commencement time, was a person approved by the Secretary under 
subsection 13.18(2) of the old Export Control (General) Order to possess an 
official marking device that is capable of being used to apply an official mark to 
prescribed plants or plant products. 
 
(2) The person is taken, at the commencement time, to have been given a written 
approval by the Secretary under paragraph 8-32(b) of this instrument to possess 
the official marking device.
