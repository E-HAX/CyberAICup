# Section 3-5 — Application of trade description

3-5  Application of trade description 
 
 If a trade description is applied to prescribed plants or plant products produced or 
prepared at an accredited property, the trade description must include at least the 
information referred to in paragraph 8-6(1)(d). 
Note: 
The trade description must also comply with section 8-7 and subsection 8-8(2).
