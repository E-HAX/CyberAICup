# Section 12-12 — Bulk vessel approval suspended before commencement time

12-12  Bulk vessel approval suspended before commencement time 
 
(1) This section applies if: 
 
(a) an authorised officer had suspended a vessel approval under 
subsection 39.1 of the old Export Control (Plants) Order; and 
 
(b) the suspension was in force immediately before the commencement time.
