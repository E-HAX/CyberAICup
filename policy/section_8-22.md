# Section 8-22 — Persons who may manufacture official marks for plants or plant products

8-22  Persons who may manufacture official marks for plants or plant products 
 
 A person may manufacture a kind of official mark for plants or plant products 
only if the Secretary has given the person a written approval to manufacture that 
kind of official mark.
