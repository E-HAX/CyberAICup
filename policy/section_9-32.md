# Section 9-32 — Certain approved containers must be sealed with tamper evident seal

9-32  Certain approved containers must be sealed with tamper evident seal 
 
 If an authorised officer approves a container and it is not intended to load the 
container immediately after the container has been inspected, an authorised 
officer must seal the container with a tamper evident seal.
