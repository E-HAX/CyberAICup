# Section 2-9 — Variation of conditions of exemption—matters to which Secretary must have

2-9  Variation of conditions of exemption—matters to which Secretary must have 
regard 
 
 For the purposes of subsection 58(3) of the Act, a matter to which the Secretary 
must have regard is whether varying a condition of an exemption in relation to 
relevant goods will ensure that one or more objects of the Act will be met in 
relation to the goods.
