# Section 8-20 — Purpose of this Division

8-20  Purpose of this Division 
 
 For the purposes of subsection 255(2) of the Act, this Division makes provision 
for and in relation to certain matters relating to official marks specified in 
Division 1 of this Part for plants or plant products that are intended to be 
exported. 
Note: 
A person may commit an offence or be liable to a civil penalty if the person engages in 
conduct that contravenes a provision in this Division (see section 258 of the Act). Other 
provisions in Division 3 of Part 3 of Chapter 8 of the Act provide offences and civil 
penalty provisions relating to false, misleading or deceptive official marks.
