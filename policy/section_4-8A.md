# Section 4-8A — Conditions relating to treatment of prescribed plants or plant products

4-8A  Conditions relating to treatment of prescribed plants or plant products 
 
 If the export operations carried out at a registered establishment are, or include, 
operations to treat prescribed plants or plant products: 
 
(a) the operations must be safe and effective; and 
 
(b) the treatment must meet importing country requirements relating to the 
export operations and the plants or plant products; and 
 
(c) if the treatment involves the application of a chemical such as an 
insecticide or a fumigant—the use of the chemical must be lawful.
