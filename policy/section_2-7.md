# Section 2-7 — Conditions of exemption—matters to which Secretary must have regard

2-7  Conditions of exemption—matters to which Secretary must have regard 
 
 For the purposes of subsection 55(2) of the Act, a matter to which the Secretary 
must have regard is whether imposing a condition on an exemption in relation to 
relevant goods will ensure that one or more objects of the Act will be met in 
relation to the goods.
