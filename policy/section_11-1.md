# Section 11-1 — Purpose of this Part

11-1  Purpose of this Part 
 
 For the purposes of subsections 408(1) and (2) of the Act, this Part makes 
provision for and in relation to the retention of records in relation to plants or 
plant products. 
Note: 
A person may commit an offence of strict liability if the person is required to retain a 
record in accordance with a provision of this Part and the person fails to comply with 
the requirement (see subsection 408(3) of the Act).
