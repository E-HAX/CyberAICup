# Section 8-4 — Additional or corrected information

8-4  Additional or corrected information 
 
 For the purposes of paragraph 244(1)(b) of the Act, a person who has given a 
notice of intention to export a consignment of prescribed plants or plant products 
must comply with subsection 244(2) of the Act if there are reasonable grounds to 
suspect that the integrity of the plants or plant products cannot be ensured.
