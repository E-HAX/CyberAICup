# Section 12-10 — Marine surveyor’s certificate issued in relation to bulk vessel before

12-10  Marine surveyor’s certificate issued in relation to bulk vessel before 
commencement time 
 
(1) This section applies if: 
 
(a) a marine surveyor had issued a certificate in relation to a bulk vessel under 
subsection 33.2 of the old Export Control (Plants) Order; and 
 
(b) the certificate had not been revoked before the commencement time. 
 
(2) The certificate continues to have effect after the commencement time as if it 
were a marine surveyor’s certificate for the bulk vessel issued under 
subsection 11-16(2) of this instrument. 
 
(3) If the certificate was issued in paper form, and the marine surveyor had not given 
the certificate and a copy of the certificate to the master of the bulk vessel before 
the commencement time, the marine surveyor must do so as soon as practicable 
after the commencement time.
