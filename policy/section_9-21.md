# Section 9-21 — Directions to deal with non-compliance with the Act etc.

9-21  Directions to deal with non-compliance with the Act etc. 
 
 For the purposes of item 8 of the table in subsection 305(1) of the Act: 
 
(a) each person (a relevant person) referred to in column 1 of an item in the 
following table is prescribed; and 
 
(b) each ground referred to in column 2 of that item is prescribed in relation to 
the relevant person prescribed by that item.
